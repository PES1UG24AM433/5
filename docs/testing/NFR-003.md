## Evaluator walkthrough and validation checks

- Browse/search menu, add items, change quantity, place an order, view order history, and track status. The checkout return-from-login issue below was observed and fixed.
- Checked estimated total: 2 × ₹12.35 wraps + 1 × ₹4.50 juice = ₹29.20.
- Incorrect login displayed “Invalid email or password.”
- Checkout validation was exercised against the isolated test backend:

| Case | Observed response |
|---|---|
| Menu item made unavailable after selection | “UX Validation Wrap is currently unavailable. Please remove it from your cart.” |
| Quantity 0 | “Each item must have a valid product ID and a positive whole-number quantity.” from API validation; frontend cart validation says “Each cart quantity must be a positive whole number.” |
| Empty cart | “Your cart is empty. Add items before checkout.” |

## Findings and corrective actions

1. **Checkout context lost after login (fixed).** A guest clicking “Place order” was sent to login, then successful login always navigated to the menu. `App.jsx` now stores the intended `/cart` return route for checkout login and restores it after successful login. The evaluator repeated the guest-checkout/login step and confirmed the cart and estimated total were still visible at `/cart`.
2. **Participant evidence is incomplete.** P1's reported completion is retrospective and help/problems were not recorded. Conduct uncoached sessions with representative students and record outcomes contemporaneously; do not infer missing observations.

## Test data and privacy

The walkthrough used synthetic items `UX Test Wrap` (₹12.35), `UX Test Juice` (₹4.50), and `UX Test Pasta` (₹8.00), and a synthetic student account. No real student information or credentials are recorded here. The test account, orders, and menu items were removed from the isolated database after testing.
